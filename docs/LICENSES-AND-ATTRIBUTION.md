# Data sources, licences, attribution

To be completed by the pipeline lane before any snapshot is published. Every
source below needs: the licence as published by the source (quoted, dated,
linked), what it permits for a paid app that bundles a derived dataset, the
attribution text the app must show and where, and any share-alike obligation
on the derived snapshot.

used until it can be. Nobody is contacted to ask.
